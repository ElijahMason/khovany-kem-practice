Mile 0

Keep the width

Khovany Kem runs the project and the cadence. He paints the work as a road. You stand on a mile, you can read the next one, and the lane does not shrink under you.

A process you can walk.

01

Name the work

One sentence for the outcome. If it needs a paragraph, it is not ready to paint on the lane.

02

Put one name on the step

One person steers that step. Help can ride along. A second name that can shrug does not.

03

Post the queue on the shoulder

Open work, the owner, and the next decision sit where the lane can read them. A private message is off the road.

04

Walk the room

Desks and hardware are part of the stripe. Khovany Kem places them so the path matches the miles. He does not run a furniture install.

05

Stop and write the decision

A stop that ends in talk is idling. Write what changes, who owns it, and when the queue will show it. Then leave.
