Use

Khovany Kem runs a project against these four rows. They are short because people have to hold them while they work.

No Standard It has failed when
01 One owner per step. One name can ask for help. That name cannot hand the step to a group and leave. Two names sit on the step, or none does.
02 A visible queue. Open work, the owner, and the next decision are where the people doing the work can read them. The line lives in a message or a hallway.
03 A meeting that ends in a decision. The last line names the change, the owner, and when the queue will show it. People leave with a recap and no sentence.
04 A room that matches the process. The path, the desks, and the hardware follow the map. The map names one path and the room forces another.

How to use the rows

Pick a step that is stuck. Read the four rows against it. Fix the row that fails. Then read the queue again.
