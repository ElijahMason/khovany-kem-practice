Mile 3

Road code

Khovany Kem posts four articles. They are short because a lane has to hold them while it moves.

Art. 1

One name

Paint one name on the step. That person can ask for help. They cannot hand the step to a group and coast. Two names means you split the step or you choose.

Art. 2

A posted queue

The shoulder shows open work, the owner, and the next decision. People who do the work can read it without asking. A hallway deal is not posted.

Art. 3

A stop writes a decision

Open against the queue. Close against the log. The decision is a sentence: what changes, who owns the change, and when the queue will show it. If you cannot write it, write the missing fact and its owner.

Art. 4

The room matches the road

The path, the desks, and the hardware follow the map. When a step moves, the thing that step needs moves with it. A true map in a contrary room teaches people to ignore the map.
